WHAT THE SITTING ADDED
----------------------

Four slots, and nothing else. No class, no enum, no annotation, no aggregate,
no equals_expression, no is_a edge, and no fact. Three existing classes each
gained one or two slot names in their `slots:` list; no existing slot was
renamed, re-ranged, re-homed or re-described. The schema description gained one
paragraph saying that session four has begun.

  free_delivery_above         Business            decimal, unit GBP
  location_minimum_flavours   InternalLocation    integer
  item_reorder_level          BoughtItem          decimal
  item_order_quantity         BoughtItem          decimal

  21 classes, unchanged
 105 slots, was 101
   0 facts, unchanged

Each carries a slot_uri under the sorella: prefix, matching its own name, as
every other slot in the map does.


WHICH RULES THESE ARE, AND WHY THESE FOUR
-----------------------------------------

§3.4 holds fifty-one rules. Four of them are named here and forty-seven are
not. The four were chosen because between them they cover both halves of the
thing being tested — that a rule can be carried half in the graph and half in
the kernel — and because two of them have a dated change with a reason written
beside it, which is what a definition change needs to be replayed at all.

  §3.4, ordering    "Pistachio paste | reorder at 2 tins, order 10 | Dan |
                    Feb 2026 — was reorder at 2, order 6. Changed after the
                    price rise, to buy ahead"
                    -> item_reorder_level, item_order_quantity

  §3.4, cabinet     "Minimum flavours in a cabinet | 12 | Marina |
                    Nov 2025 — was 16 at Cotham. Cut to reduce end-of-day
                    waste; Aoife still disputes it"
                    -> location_minimum_flavours

  §3.4, wholesale   "Free delivery above | 120 pounds | Marina |
                    Apr 2026 — was 80. Fuel and van servicing"
                    -> free_delivery_above

Not one of those numbers is in this file. A slot is a place for a number and
the number is a dated assertion in the log, which is the split the design turns
on: a parameter lives in the kernel with its history, and the graph names it.
This version does the naming half. The four numbers, their earlier values and
their dates are a later act.


CHOICES THE PROFILE DOES NOT MAKE
---------------------------------

 1. No unit slot beside the two ordering numbers.
    §3.4 writes "reorder at 2 tins" and §1.5 already says pistachio paste is
    counted in tins, so item_counted_in carries the unit and the level is a
    bare number in it. A unit slot beside the level would state the same thing
    twice and let the two disagree.
    Rejected: item_reorder_level_unit, and a value_ref to Unit as every other
    quantity slot in the map has. The difference from those is that a movement
    or a recipe line names its own unit on the document, and a reorder level
    does not — the level is written in the unit the item is already counted in,
    on the same line of the same table.
    The cost is named rather than absorbed: §3.4's Base 50 stabiliser row is
    "reorder at 4 bags, order 1 carton", which is the counted unit for one
    number and the ordered unit for the other. Under this choice nothing in the
    map says which of the two a given item_order_quantity is in. It is written
    into the slot's own description so a reader meets it, and it is why that
    description does not claim a single unit basis.

 2. free_delivery_above stands on Business, not on WholesaleAccount.
    §3.4 states it once, in the wholesale table, with no per-account exception —
    unlike credit terms, which the same table gives standard and then lists five
    exceptions to, and which already sits on WholesaleAccount as credit_terms.
    Rejected: a slot on WholesaleAccount defaulted per account. It would invent
    thirty-one facts where the business states one.
    Business has one row, so this is the map's first slot whose subject is the
    company itself and whose value is a number rather than a name.

 3. location_minimum_flavours is on InternalLocation and not on a Cabinet class.
    §1.3's tables put the two serve cabinets among the internal places and give
    them no columns the other internal places lack. A class for them would be a
    class with one slot, which the 1 Sep rule says is one class with a field.
    Rejected: a Cabinet class. Also rejected: putting it on Location, which
    would let an outside place — a cafe, a bin — carry a minimum.
    The cost: nothing in the map says only a cabinet may carry one, so a chest
    freezer with a minimum flavour count is a map-valid absurdity. That is the
    same shape as every other optional slot on InternalLocation, including
    location_temperature.

 4. Its range is integer where the other three are decimal.
    A flavour count is flavours. The other three are money, or a quantity in a
    unit that can be fractional.

 5. valid_from is unchanged from v1: 2026-06-15T00:00:00Z.
    v2 is not the world changing and it is not a correction of an error. It is
    the same period described further, and under the two-axis rule that is
    valid_from unchanged and a later sealed_at. resolve_version filters on
    valid_from and orders on sealed_at, so two versions sharing a valid_from are
    not ambiguous — the later seal wins from its sealed_at onward, and a reader
    asking as of a moment before it still gets v1.


WHAT WAS DELIBERATELY NOT WRITTEN
---------------------------------

No number. Not the 12, not the 120, not the 2 or the 10, and not the 16, the 80
or the 6 they each replaced. A number in a map file is a fact living outside the
log, and `seal` strips a draft's facts for that reason.

No aggregate and no equals_expression naming any of the four. Nothing can
execute a parameter yet, and notation that moves nothing reads as capability the
system does not have. When something can execute one, the construct that says so
is a change to this map and gets its own version.

No required: true on any of the four. Seventy-six of the eighty bought items
have no level, most internal places are not cabinets, and the business has one
row; three of the four are absent far more often than they are present.

Forty-seven of §3.4's fifty-one rules. They fall into shapes nothing can yet
hold — a refusal (no sorbet in a cake, minimum order four pans), a schedule
(van runs Tue/Thu/Sat May to September), a duration (a mix stands 24 hours), a
supplier's own term (Terra Nostra's 400 pounds for free carriage) — and each is
an OPEN line rather than a slot invented to park it.

§3.5 entirely. Nine numbers each computed two ways by two people, both in use,
neither wrong. A slot cannot hold two answers and the map has no construct for
a disagreement.
