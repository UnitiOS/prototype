Provenance note for business/draft.yaml — Marlow Gelato, the v1 draft.

There was no conversation. The interview is deferred for this PoC (DECISIONS.md,
29 Aug), so the map was authored by hand from business/profile.md and from
nothing else, and this note stands where a transcript would. It records what was
read, what was decided where the profile does not decide, and what was rejected,
so that "why does the map say this" is answerable from a file rather than from
whoever wrote it.

Nothing outside profile.md was used as a source. DECISIONS.md and NEXT.md were
read as instructions about how to author, never as facts about the shop.


WHAT BECAME WHAT
----------------

The profile's own read order was followed: section 4 things became classes,
section 5 attributes became slots, section 6 relationships became slots whose
range is another class, section 7 movements became classes, and section 13 is
the whole of the facts.

  section 1, 3, 8   descriptions on the classes, in the shop's own words
  section 4         nine classes: Material, Supplier, Flavour, Base, MixBatch,
                    Pan, Location, StockCount, Sale
  section 5         the attribute slots, each carrying the profile's unit where
                    the profile fixes one
  section 6         eleven slots whose range is a class, listed below
  section 7         five more classes under one parent, listed below
  section 10        six stated_rule annotations, verbatim, on the slot each rule
                    is about
  section 11        two readings of "the vanilla" as two slots on one subject;
                    two meanings of "batch" as MixBatch and GoodsReceived
  section 12        gaps, kept as gaps: no slot was written for any of them
  section 13        127 facts, and no fact comes from anywhere else

Three classes are not in section 4 and are there because section 6 or the
avoidance of any_of requires them:

  Person      section 6 states "Mix batch was made by a person", and a
              relationship's range has to be a class
  Unit        section 6 states "Material is measured in Unit"
  StockItem   the common parent of Material and Pan, so that "Stock count
              counted Material or Pan" is one class-ranged slot

Section 6, relationship by relationship, so the coverage can be checked:

  Flavour is made from Base          flavour_base        -> Base
  Mix batch is a batch of Base       mix_base            -> Base
  Mix batch was made by a person     mix_made_by         -> Person
  Pan holds Flavour                  pan_flavour         -> Flavour
  Pan was filled from Mix batch      pan_mix             -> MixBatch
  Pan sits in Location               stock_location      -> Location
  Material is bought from Supplier   material_supplier   -> Supplier
  Material is measured in Unit       material_unit       -> Unit
  Stock count counted Material/Pan   count_of            -> StockItem
  Stock count happened at Location   count_location      -> Location
  Sale was of Flavour                sale_flavour        -> Flavour


WHO WOULD HAVE WRITTEN THESE FACTS
----------------------------------

All 127 facts are one act: the count Marta and Dan took on the evening of
31 August 2025, described to an interviewer and sealed. That is the onboarding
writer named by the 30 Aug decision — the interview and `seal` — so they belong
in one intent and there is nothing here a generated form or an agent would have
written instead. No daily entry, no correction, no derived total.

The test applied while writing: if a fact would have arrived on a Friday from an
invoice, on a Sunday from Dan's paper log, or as a correction, it does not
belong in this draft. Nothing in section 13 arrives that way.


CHOICES THE PROFILE DOES NOT MAKE
---------------------------------

Each of these was underdetermined by profile.md. The plainest option was taken,
and the alternative is recorded so it can be taken back.

1. Section 7's movements are five classes, not nine and not none.
   Four of the nine rows are already section 4 things — "base made" is a
   MixBatch, "pan filled" is a Pan, "scoop sold" is a Sale, "stock counted" is a
   StockCount — so only five are new: GoodsReceived, PanMoved, PanPulled,
   ThrownOut, TastingGiven. They share one StockMovement parent carrying what
   section 7's table states: what moved, how much, out of where, into where,
   when, and who recorded it.
   Rejected: omitting section 7 entirely. The done condition names sections 4
   and 6 only, but it also sets a floor — at least what an ERP inventory module
   holds — and an inventory module with no stock movements does not clear it.
   Also rejected: one class with a movement_kind enum. The three movements
   nobody records are three of these five, and as enum values they would be
   three strings rather than three things a reader can see are empty.

2. StockItem carries stock_location, so a material and a pan use one slot to say
   where they sit.
   Rejected: separate material_location and pan_location slots, leaving
   StockItem with nothing in it. Section 8 states location as a property of
   stock in general ("one place stock sits"), and a parent that exists only to
   be the range of one slot is an artefact of avoiding any_of rather than
   something the business says.

3. The pan-table facts state no confidence at all.
   Section 13 gives a Confidence column to the nineteen materials and none to
   the ten flavours, so the six estimated quantities record `low`, the thirteen
   counted ones record `high`, and every other fact records NULL.
   Rejected: marking the flavour rows `high` from section 13's prose, "the
   freezers were counted pan by pan". It is true, but the business wrote a
   confidence beside nineteen numbers and not beside these, and a default claims
   more than anyone said — the same reasoning as the 30 Aug decision.

4. Location and Unit receive a name fact; Supplier, Base, Person, Pan,
   MixBatch, StockCount, Sale and the five movements receive nothing.
   Section 13's Location and Unit columns name chiller, dry store, litre, kg and
   piece, so those five entities are named by the profile as well as pointed at.
   Rejected: leaving them as bare URIs minted only because a fact points at
   them. Then "38 litres in the chiller" would be readable nowhere but in the
   URI, and a URI is not a name.

5. The flavour called Dark chocolate is uniti:dark_chocolate_flavour, while the
   eight kilograms of chocolate in the dry store is uniti:dark_chocolate.
   The shop uses one phrase for two things, exactly as section 11 describes for
   "the vanilla" and "batch". It is the only collision in section 13.
   Rejected: prefixing every URI with its class — uniti:material_sugar,
   uniti:flavour_vanilla. Tidier, and it hides that the collision happened.

6. A name slot is `identifier: true` only where section 4 names one field that
   identifies the thing: Material, Supplier, Flavour, Base, Location, Unit,
   Person. Pan is not — section 4 says the number is written on tape and section
   12 says numbers are reused, so pan_number is an ordinary slot. MixBatch,
   StockCount and Sale are identified by two fields each, so none of their slots
   is an identifier either.
   Rejected: no identifiers anywhere. That would drop the one place the profile
   is explicit about identity, and it would lose the finding that four of the
   nine things have no single identifier the shop can state.

7. The six flavours with a sticker over them carry a name and nothing else.
   Section 13 names peach, tiramisu, hazelnut, raspberry sorbet, black sesame
   and gingerbread as having had no stock.
   Rejected: a quantity of zero. Nobody weighed them; "had no stock" is a
   sentence, and 0.0 kg is a measurement. Also rejected: leaving them out
   entirely, which would lose six of the sixteen recipes the shop has.

8. Section 10's shrinkage rule is a stated_rule on count_quantity, and there is
   no shrinkage slot.
   The month-end count is where section 9 says the shop settles its numbers, so
   the threshold hangs there. v1 carries no derived rule, per the 29 Aug
   decision, so how shrinkage is computed is not in this map at all — only that
   above five percent means something is wrong.
   Rejected: a shrinkage slot carrying Marta's arithmetic in prose. It would be
   a derived quantity in a version that is supposed to have none, and it would
   spend the definition change before it happens.

9. The rule on flavour_kilograms still says "below five litres" while the slot
   is in kilograms. Left as it stands: section 12 records that nobody has ever
   said which unit that rule means, and rewriting it in kilograms would answer
   the question on the shop's behalf.


WHAT WAS DELIBERATELY NOT WRITTEN
---------------------------------

Section 12's gaps are gaps. There is no slot for density per flavour, for actual
overrun, for actual scoop weight, or for a supplier lot number, and there is no
history before 1 September 2025. A map that answers those would have invented
business.

Location is folded into the pan slot names — flavour_pans_on_display and
flavour_pans_in_storage — rather than being a dimension of one quantity, which
is the untidiness the 30 Aug decision accepted on purpose. "How much vanilla is
in the shop" is flavour_kilograms; "how much is out front" is a pan count, and
the two do not add up to each other.

No pan is named. Section 13 weighed twenty-one of them and wrote down ten
totals, so twenty-one individuals would mean twenty-one invented weights, and
section 12 says the numbers on the tape are reused.

Thirty-five of the forty-five slots receive no fact at all: every price, every
pack size, the shelf life, the base of each flavour, whether it is in rotation,
and everything about a mix batch, a pan, a sale, a count and a movement. At
onboarding the shop stated a balance and not a history, and the empty slots are
the honest shape of that.


WHAT THE RENDERS SHOWED
-----------------------

`gen-owl --no-use-native-uris` and `gen-erdiagram` both exit 0. The OWL carries
eighteen classes and all seven is_a edges, and the six stated_rule annotations
survive as triples. Two things worth knowing before anyone looks at either:

The OWL carries the whole facts block as one 12,000-character string literal,
because facts live under `annotations` and gen-owl renders annotations as
triples on the ontology. A quarter of the TTL is the log. It disappears when the
draft is sealed — `seal` strips `facts` — so this is a property of looking at a
draft, not of looking at a version.

The Mermaid diagram flattens is_a, so each of the five movement subclasses
repeats its parent's six relationships: twenty of the thirty-two edges are the
same six seen five times. Readable, but a change to StockMovement in v2 will
show up six times in the diff rather than once.

The slot-level `unit` metadata does not reach the OWL at all. Kilograms, litres,
pounds and days are in the YAML and in nothing generated from it so far.
