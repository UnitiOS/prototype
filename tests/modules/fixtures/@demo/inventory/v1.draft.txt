Demonstration content.

This module pins @demo/catalog@v1 and says nothing about what an ingredient
is; it only points at one. That is the whole of the composition claim: the
predicate `cat:ingredient_label` belongs to the catalog, `inv:movement_quantity`
belongs here, and a reader that holds both sees one graph.

The quantity is signed rather than paired with an in/out word, so that the
arithmetic anybody wants — how much is here — is a sum and not a case
analysis. Whether that survives contact with returns and corrections is a
question for a later version.

No level, no total, no "on hand" here. This module states what happened.
