Transcript, @demo/inventory v3. Demonstration content — none of this is real.

Joko, who receives deliveries, was read the three bindings core's Flow asks for
and asked whether a stock movement has them.

Amount — yes, that is the quantity, and he was firm that it stays signed.
"Twenty in and twenty out is not forty, it is nothing." Which is the reason the
role asks for one signed number rather than a quantity and a direction.

Affects — yes, the ingredient. Asked whether it could ever be something that is
not an ingredient, he said no, not here. So the range is narrowed from Resource
to Ingredient, and this module is a bakery's rather than a general one. Written
down as a decision rather than a habit: leaving it at Resource would have made
it usable by the textile mill down the road.

Moment — this one took a while. He first said the date was on the delivery note
and asked why the system needed it as well, since the note is filed. Then he
described the case that matters: a note for the twelfth found in the middle of
July. The day it moved and the day we learned of it are different days, and he
wants both.

That is exactly the split the kernel already makes, so `movement_happened_on`
stays and fills `moment`. Worth recording that until this version nothing read
it at all — two hundred-odd statements under a predicate no rule touched. It
was not wrong, it was unread.

`movement_location` was raised and deliberately left out of any role. Core has
nothing for a place. So an application that groups by location is pinned to
this module and cannot swap it — which is a fair trade and better said out loud
than discovered later.
