The inventory module was brought to the recording standard, and moved its pin
to the catalogue's v4, which had been brought to it first.

Which way does a quantity run? Positive is stock coming in, negative is stock
going out; the module always said so in prose, and now says it in the field
every reader can check.

What day is the happened-on date? The day the goods moved. A delivery note
found in July for a delivery in June is dated June here; the log keeps the
July as the day it learned of it.

The bakery's words — Mutasi stok, Jumlah, Tanggal kejadian — are kept as
labels. Nothing was renamed and no predicate changed.
