Transcript, @demo/catalog v3. Demonstration content — none of this is real.

Rina, who buys for the bakery, was asked one question: is an ingredient the
same kind of thing as a category or a supplier?

She said no, and the reason was practical rather than philosophical. You can
have five kilos of flour and you cannot have five kilos of Dry Goods. Flour
runs out. A category does not.

That is the distinction core calls Resource, so Ingredient mixes it in and the
other two do not. Nothing else changed: the predicates are the ones v2 already
had, and every fact stated under them still stands.

What this buys, which Rina did not need to care about: an inventory module can
now be written by somebody who has never heard of a bakery. It says it moves a
Resource, and the log says flour is one.

The `ingredient_unit` slot was discussed and left alone. It records that flour
is counted in kilos and eggs in pieces, and nothing reads it — core's `amount`
has no unit binding yet, so two modules moving the same ingredient in different
units would produce a wrong total quietly. Noted and not fixed here.
