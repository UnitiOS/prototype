Transcript, @demo/catalog v4. Demonstration content — none of this is real.

The catalogue was brought to the recording standard every module is now held
to. Rina was asked three things the earlier versions never wrote down.

What is a reorder level counted in? In whatever the ingredient is counted in:
kilos for flour, litres for milk. So its unit is the ingredient's own.

Does it ever go up or down by itself? No. It is a level somebody sets, not a
movement of anything.

And what does she call each thing? Her words — Bahan, Pemasok, Batas pesan
ulang — are kept beside the English names, so the studio shows them to her
while the module itself is written the way every other module is.

Nothing was renamed and no predicate changed.
