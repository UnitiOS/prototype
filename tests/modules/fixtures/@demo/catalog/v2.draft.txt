Transcript, @demo/catalog v2.

Demonstration content.

Two changes, and they are different in kind.

`ingredient_label` is renamed to `ingredient_name`. The predicate is untouched
— both versions say `cat:ingredient_label` — so this is a change of wording and
not of meaning, and every fact already in the log is still a fact about the
same thing. A reader of v1 and a reader of v2 disagree about what to call the
slot and about nothing else.

`ingredient_reorder_level` is new. It is stated by a person, never derived: it
is somebody's judgement about how low is too low, and the log keeps who said
so and from when. @demo/stock reads it as a parameter.
