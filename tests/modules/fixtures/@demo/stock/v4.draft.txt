Transcript, @demo/stock v4. Demonstration content — none of this is real.

The application was brought to the recording standard after the modules under
it were. It pins @demo/catalog v4 and @rival/stock-moves v2.

Its columns were renamed to start with `stock_`, as every column of one table
now does. A column's predicate is kept, so a rename loses nothing; the rules
naming the columns were renamed with them.

Every column is a level in the ingredient's own unit: what is on hand, the
reorder level, and the headroom between them. The bakery's words for the
table and its columns — Stok bahan, Stok tersedia, Batas pesan ulang — are
kept as labels.

The examples are v3's, told again in the rival's new words and the table's
new column names. Their numbers did not change.
