# The seed: everything Tuesday 16 June 2026 touches

The enumeration, written **before** anything is submitted. `NEXT.md`, stage
**substrate**, done condition 1: applying the selection rule to the profile
yields a list, and its size is then a measured fact rather than a guess.

Nothing here has been run. This file is the specification the seeding script
transcribes, and it is committed so that the script can be read against it.

## The selection rule

> Everything Tuesday 16 June 2026 touches — profile §4.3 — **plus the opening
> position of each thing it touches**, from §4.1's count of the previous
> morning.

16 June is milestone one's own day (`ROADMAP.md`), so this seed is
milestone-one work and not a detour. Anyone can apply the rule to the profile
and arrive at the same set.

**Nothing is dropped for being awkward.** A row that cannot be recorded is
written up in *Findings* below, never skipped silently.

## The conventions this enumeration commits to

Four decisions had to be taken before a row could be written down. Each is
stated here rather than inside the script, so that a reader can disagree with
one without reading any code.

**One movement per line of paper.** §3.2 writes the van into the middle of
every delivery — *Holding freezer → The van → The wholesale account* — but the
van sheet and the delivery note are one document, written once, at loading or
at the first drop. Recording a loading leg and a drop leg would invent two
events from one line. So a movement runs from where the paper says it came to
where the paper says it went, and **the van is named only where the profile
itself puts something in it**: the two pans loaded on Saturday and not dropped,
the third-full pan that came back from Cleeve, and the empty pans collected.

**A quantity is the number on the paper, or nothing.** No conversion, no
rounding, no fraction arithmetic. "1 half pan", "about a third full", "¾" and
"5 sealed, 1 open" are **not** numbers this seed invents a value for: the row
is recorded with no `movement_quantity` and the words go in the note. §3.5 says
Marina counts a part pan as a half and Aoife counts it as a pan, so a business
with two answers has none, and a seed that picks one is the script taking a
view. This lands on the standing `OPEN.md` T3 — *what a movement with no stated
quantity means when summed*.

**A place is the finest one the paper distinguishes.** The Cotham van sheet
does not say which of the two back freezers a pan went into — §3.2: *"Nothing
anywhere says which of the two freezers a pan went into"* — so those rows name
`loc_cotham_hill`. Where the paper names a well or a shelf, the row names it.

**Master data is not in this seed.** The rule selects movements and opening
positions. Item names, units, suppliers and prices are §1.5/§1.6 facts the
profile dates almost nowhere, and dating them by the adoption date is exactly
the defect that rejected the working log (`NEXT.md`). Every entity below is
minted by being named — one `uniti:uri` row and nothing else — until something
dated says more about it. Six parameter values arrive separately, through
`scripts/state_stage3.sh`, each with the date the profile gives.

## How to read a row

| Column | Means |
|---|---|
| `#` | the row's number in this enumeration |
| `subject` | the URI, minus the `sorella:` prefix |
| `out of` → `into` | `movement_out_of`, `movement_into`. `—` is an empty slot, not a missing one |
| `what` | `movement_ingredient`, or `movement_flavour` + `movement_format` |
| `qty` | `movement_quantity` + `movement_unit`. `—` where the profile gives no number |

Every movement below carries `happened_on` and `valid_from` of the day it
happened, and `entity_class=StockMovement`. `movement_kind` and
`movement_batch` are empty throughout: the log holds no `MovementKind` and no
`Batch` entity, and minting one to fill a picker would state a thing nobody
has. `written_by` is filled only where the profile names who signed.
